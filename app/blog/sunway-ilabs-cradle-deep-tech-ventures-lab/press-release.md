# Our Deep Tech Ventures Lab journey: from launch to the stage

*By Audrey Aui Yong, Founder and CEO of Tsuin AI*

When Tsuin AI was selected for the Startup Deep Tech Ventures Lab (DTVL), I knew it would be a big few months. What I did not fully expect was how much ground we would cover: a national launch, months of building, a summit floor full of investors, and a stage with our name on the screen. This is that journey, told the way I lived it.

![The Startup Deep Tech Ventures Lab launch, with the cohort and partners on stage.](/blog/dtvl/01-launch.webp)
*The official launch of the Startup Deep Tech Ventures Lab (NURTURING THE INNOVATION ECOSYSTEM), backed by Sunway iLabs, Cradle, and MYStartup.*

## Chapter one: the launch (7 August 2025)

DTVL is a six-month co-creation programme (July to December 2025) launched by Sunway iLabs, the innovation arm of Sunway Group, together with Cradle Fund Sdn Bhd, the national agency for early-stage startups under the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation. It is built for founders working where science, engineering, and real-world impact meet, with a clear lean toward artificial intelligence and deep tech.

We were one of 20 startups chosen from 69 applicants (an acceptance rate of roughly 29 percent). Standing in that room at the launch, surrounded by the other founders and the partners backing us, the scale of it landed. This was not a logo exercise. It was a bet on hard problems.

Matt van Leeuwen, Chief Innovation Officer of Sunway Group and CEO of Sunway iLabs, framed it well: "DTVL embodies our vision to drive deep tech innovation that delivers both commercial success and meaningful societal impact." Norman Matthieu Vanhaecke, Group CEO of Cradle, put the challenge plainly: "Deep tech is about solving real, complex challenges at scale."

![At the Sunway iLabs space, under the Inspire, Build, Scale sign.](/blog/dtvl/02-ilabs-inspire-build-scale.jpg)
*Inspire, Build, Scale. Stepping into the Sunway iLabs world at the start of the programme.*

## Chapter two: the build

The programme runs on three things: Inspire, Build, Scale. In practice that meant expert-led workshops, monthly mentorship office hours, investor-readiness sessions, and access to a serious capital network. The ecosystem around us was deep: delivery partners Deep Tech Labs (the UK accelerator backed by the University of Cambridge, ARM, and Cambridge Innovation Capital), Investible (early-stage climate-tech investors from Australia and Singapore), and CloudMile (AI and cloud), plus venture firms Gobi Partners and The Hive Southeast Asia, MRANTI, and seven Malaysian universities.

![The Startup Tech Ventures 2025 backdrop with partner logos.](/blog/dtvl/03-dtvl-backdrop-2025.jpg)
*Organised by Sunway iLabs, powered by Cradle, with strategic partners Deep Tech Labs, CloudMile, and Investible.*

The cohort spanned seven priority verticals: AI and deep tech, food and water security, health and wellness, intelligent autonomy, semiconductor applications, energy transition, and decarbonisation (carbon capture, utilisation, and storage). Being surrounded by founders tackling those problems changed how we thought about our own.

## Chapter three: the showcase at the Startup ASEAN Summit

Then came the part that tests whether you have built something real: the floor. At the Cradle Live! Startup ASEAN Summit 2025, we set up our booth and put the AI Twin in front of anyone who would stop and look.

![Demonstrating the Tsuin AI Twin live at the booth.](/blog/dtvl/04-booth-demo-ai-twin.jpg)
*Live at the booth: the AI Twin running on screen, capturing a developer's thought process.*

Our message was blunt on purpose: your code has a story, do not let it die when developers leave. Every "why" preserved. Every decision remembered. Every developer empowered.

![The tsuin.ai booth poster: Your code has a story. Don't let it die when developers leave.](/blog/dtvl/05-booth-poster.jpg)

## Chapter four: taking the stage

The last step was the pitch. On stage, our slide read "Capturing the Developer's Mind": build the leading AI Twin platform that captures developers' thought processes through human-AI interaction, prevent costly knowledge loss, and accelerate team onboarding by 70 percent in 18 months.

![Pitching Tsuin AI on stage: Capturing the Developer's Mind.](/blog/dtvl/11-pitch-stage.jpg)
*On stage at the summit. Product: AI Twin Platform. Company: Tsuin AI Sdn Bhd.*

![On stage presenting the AI Twin.](/blog/dtvl/12-pitch-closeup.jpg)
*Four months of work in a few minutes on the mic.*

Standing up there, after the launch, the workshops, and the booth, the throughline was obvious. As I said in my remarks: "Moving forward, our startup's journey is very exciting, because through the Deep Tech Ventures Lab programme we are progressing really fast."

## What's next

The programme moves toward its milestones: a pre-demo day where founders present progress to regional VCs, corporates, and policymakers, and a final demo day for a shortlist of ventures. For us, the Deep Tech Ventures Lab did exactly what a good programme should: it compressed time. We are building faster, with sharper conviction, and with a network behind us that did not exist for us a few months ago.

If you want to see what we are building, the AI Twin is at [tsuin.ai](https://tsuin.ai).
